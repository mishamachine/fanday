# Fanday Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 3 November 2026
**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

Fanday puts your teams' games in your phone's calendar and keeps them up to date. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how to delete it.

**The short version**
- You can use Fanday without signing in.
- We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
- There are no third-party ad or analytics tools in the app.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time.

## 1. Who we are

Fanday is run by B&B Project Factory ("we", "us"). For anything about privacy, email **machinemisha+fanday@gmail.com**.

Fanday is offered in the United States.

## 2. What we collect

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **The teams you follow** | When you pick teams | To put their games in your calendar and keep your teams across devices |
| **A random account ID** | When you first open the app. You get an anonymous account; no sign-in is needed | To keep your teams and devices together |
| **Your email address** | Only if you choose to sign in with Apple, Google or an email link | To sign you in and move your teams to a new phone |
| **Your name and profile picture link** | Only if you sign in with Google, which shares them with our sign-in system | Our sign-in system may store them with your account. We don't use them |
| **Your notification choices** | When you change them in Settings, for example "Sponsor messages" | To respect your choices |
| **Your device's push token** | Only if you allow notifications | To alert you when a kickoff time changes |
| **Calendar event IDs** | When Fanday adds a game to your calendar | To update or remove the events Fanday created. They are kept on your phone and may be stored with your account |
| **Sign-in records** | When you sign in or the app refreshes your session | Our sign-in system may record the IP address and device type of each session, for security |
| **App usage events**, for example "added a team" | We may record these when you use the app | To fix problems and see which features people use. Kept in our own database, not sent to analytics companies |

If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we only get Apple's relay address.

**What we don't collect:** Fanday only reads and writes events in its own "Fanday" calendar. It doesn't read, copy or upload the other events in your calendar. We don't collect your location, contacts or photos.

**Paid-link clicks:** when you tap a "paid link" (for example "Where to watch" or "Tickets"), we add one to a count for that game on that day. The count is not linked to you, and we don't store your IP address with it.

## 3. Your calendar and notifications

- Fanday asks for permission to add and update events in your calendar. On iPhone, iOS may ask you for full calendar access, which Fanday needs to update an event when a kickoff time changes.
- Fanday puts games in its own calendar called "Fanday". It only changes the events it created.
- Push notifications are optional. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.
- We send sponsor or promotional notifications only if you turn on "Sponsor messages" in Settings. It is off by default.

You can turn off calendar or notification access at any time in your phone's settings. Without calendar access, Fanday can't add games for you.

## 4. How we use your data

We use your data only to:
- run Fanday: sync games, send kickoff alerts, and keep your teams across devices;
- understand and improve the app, using our own usage records;
- keep the service secure and prevent abuse;
- answer your emails;
- meet legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising (the term California law uses). We don't build ad profiles.

## 5. Who helps us run Fanday

These providers handle data for us so we can run Fanday.

| Provider | What it does for Fanday | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (database in the United States) | Database and sign-in | Everything in section 2 |
| **Google** | Google sign-in. Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push alerts. Gmail sends sign-in emails and receives support email | Email, name and profile picture link (Google sign-in only), push token, the emails we send you and that you send us |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple. Apple Push Notification service delivers alerts on iPhone | Email or relay address (Apple sign-in only), push token |
| **Hostinger** | Hosts the fanday.bbprojectfactory.com website | Standard web request data, such as IP address, in its server logs |
| **GitHub** | Runs our scheduled schedule-update job | None of your personal data |

Sign-in emails come from a Gmail account we run (machinemisha@gmail.com), with "Fanday" as the sender name. Gmail is a standard Google account, used under Google's terms and privacy policy.

We use these providers only to run Fanday, and we choose settings that limit what they get (for example, Firebase's analytics is off). We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety. If Fanday is ever sold or merged, your data would stay under this policy, and we would tell you first.

## 6. Other services you can open from Fanday

Some links in Fanday take you to services we don't run:
- **Official team calendars:** for some leagues, such as the NBA and NHL, Fanday offers a one-tap link to the team's own calendar subscription. That calendar comes from the team or its provider, under their privacy policy.
- **Paid links and sponsors:** "Where to watch", "Tickets" and sponsor links go to partner sites. If you buy something there, the partner may pay us a commission. Their privacy policy applies once you're on their site.
- **Wikipedia:** we get schedule data from Wikipedia. Your data is not sent to Wikipedia.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Your account, teams, devices and calendar event IDs:** until you delete your account.
- **Accounts nobody uses:** we aim to delete anonymous accounts that haven't been used for 24 months.
- **The anonymous account you started with:** when you sign in, your teams move to your signed-in account. We aim to delete the old anonymous account 7 days later.
- **Usage events:** we aim to delete them after 13 months.
- **Emails you send us:** we aim to delete them 12 months after your request is dealt with.
- **Paid-link click counts:** kept, because they contain no personal data.

Today these deletions are done by us, not by an automatic process. We'll update this policy when automatic deletion is in place. If you want your data gone sooner, delete your account (section 8).

## 8. Deleting your data

- **In the app:** Settings → Delete account, then confirm. This deletes your account and everything linked to it from our database. It removes the Fanday calendar from that phone. If you used Sign in with Apple, it also revokes Fanday's access to your Apple ID.
- **Without the app:** email machinemisha+fanday@gmail.com from the address on your account, or tell us how to find your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm.

If Fanday is on more than one phone, delete the "Fanday" calendar on the other phones in your calendar settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete your data from our servers. Use one of the options above.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to:
- give you a copy of your data;
- correct it;
- delete it.

Email machinemisha+fanday@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days (45 days for California requests). We may need to confirm the request is yours. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

**California:** you have the rights to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal data, so there is nothing to opt out of. In the past 12 months we collected:
- identifiers (email, account ID, push token, and name if you used Google sign-in);
- internet or app activity (usage events and sign-in records).

All of it came from you, your device or your sign-in provider, for the purposes in section 4. We disclosed it only to the providers in section 5. We don't collect sensitive personal information. You can use an authorized agent.

**Other US states:** some states give similar rights. Email us and we'll honor them.

**Outside the US:** Fanday is offered in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your data is stored and processed in the US, and you can still use the rights above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 10. Security

- Data is encrypted in transit.
- Access to our database is restricted.
- Each user can reach only their own records.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## 11. Children

Fanday is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child under 13 has used Fanday, email us and we'll delete the data.

## 12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
