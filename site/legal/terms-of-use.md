These sources can be late, incomplete or wrong. Times change, and alerts can be delayed or fail, for example when your phone limits background activity.

So please check the official source before you rely on a time: before you travel, buy tickets or set a recording. A game with no confirmed time yet appears as an all-day "time TBC" event.
