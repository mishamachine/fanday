Delete your account

You can delete your Fanday account and the data tied to it at any time. Two ways:

1. In the app (fastest)

Open Fanday, go to Settings, then Delete account, and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away. If you signed in with Apple, the app asks Apple to confirm first, and nothing is deleted unless that succeeds.

2. By email

If you can't open the app, email machinemisha+fanday@gmail.com with the subject Fanday: delete my account. Send it from the email address on your account so we can match it. We may reply to check it's you.

We delete the account within 30 days of confirming it's you, and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details, including your email.
  • The teams you follow, your device and notification settings, and the usage stats linked to your account.
  • The Fanday calendar the app made on your phone is removed when you delete in the app. If you delete by email, remove it yourself in your calendar app.

What is not deleted

  • Events already copied to calendars the app wrote to, if you keep them. Delete them in your calendar app.
  • Counts of affiliate-link clicks per game. They are not tied to any person.

Deleting can't be undone. You can use Fanday again without an account at any time.
