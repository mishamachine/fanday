Delete your account

You can delete your Fanday account and the data tied to it at any time. This follows section 8 of our Privacy Policy.

In the app

Open Fanday, go to Settings, then Delete account, and confirm. This deletes your account and everything linked to it from our database. It removes the Fanday calendar from that phone. If you used Sign in with Apple, it also revokes Fanday's access to your Apple ID.

Without the app

Email machinemisha+fanday@gmail.com with the subject Fanday: delete my account, from the address on your account, or tell us how to find your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm.

More than one phone

If Fanday is on more than one phone, delete the "Fanday" calendar on the other phones in your calendar settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete your data from our servers. Use one of the options above.
