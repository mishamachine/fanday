Delete your account

You can delete your Fanday account and the data tied to it at any time. This follows section 8 of our Privacy Policy.

In the app

Open Fanday, go to Settings, then Delete account, and confirm. This deletes all your Fanday data from our database. If you created your sign-in through Fanday and none of our other apps holds data for you, we delete the sign-in too. Otherwise the sign-in stays, because our other apps use it; you can delete it from those apps. If you signed in with Apple, we also tell Apple to disconnect Fanday.

Signed out: This deletes everything linked to this device from our database and stops its device code working. Either way, it removes the Fanday calendar from that phone.

Without the app

Email machinemisha+fanday@gmail.com with the subject Fanday: delete my account, from the address on your account, or tell us how to find your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm.

More than one phone

If Fanday is on more than one phone, delete the "Fanday" calendar on the other phones in your calendar settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete your data from our servers. Use one of the options above.
