Privacy

We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

In short

  • You can use Fanday without an account. Signing in is optional.
  • If you sign in, we hold your email (optional) so your teams follow you across devices.
  • We keep the teams you follow, and basic usage stats from our own systems, to run and improve the app.
  • No third-party ad or analytics tools. No tracking across other companies' apps or sites.
  • Affiliate clicks are counted per game, not per person.
  • Marketing email only if you opt in.
  • Schedule data comes from public sources, including Wikipedia (CC BY-SA).

You can delete your account and data at any time: how to delete your account.
