Your games. Your calendar. That's it.

Pick the teams you follow. Fanday keeps your phone and calendars synced to every game, with the kickoff time, where to watch and the stadium, and updates them if the time changes. Free.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How it works

  1. Pick a league, then your teams.
  2. Allow calendar access once.
  3. Done. Each game shows up with a reminder before kickoff, and we send an alert if the time moves.

Privacy

You can use Fanday with just the teams you pick. You can also add an email, optional, to save your teams across devices. We collect that email, the teams you follow and basic usage stats from our own systems, to make the app better. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers. Schedule information comes from public sources, including Wikipedia (CC BY-SA). See the privacy policy, or how to delete your account.
